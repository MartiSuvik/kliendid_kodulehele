1. The "Hero Reveal" Entrance
As the page loads, have the main heading ("Tõstame Teie ettevõtte päringuid...") fade in and slide up by about 20px.

Why: It creates a focal point immediately and feels more polished than a static "pop."

Timing: 0.8s duration with a cubic-bezier ease-out.

2. Video Thumbnail "Pulse"
The play button on your hero video should have a subtle, infinite "ripple" or pulse effect behind it.

Why: It signals to the user that the element is interactive without them having to hover first.

3. Benefit Card "Lift & Glow"
In the "Jah, kas see on üldse väärt?" section, apply a hover effect to the three cards (Nähtavus, Usaldusväärsus, Prognoositavus).

The Motion: On hover, the card should move -10px on the Y-axis and its shadow should soften and expand.

Visual: The icon inside could change from its default color to your brand's primary blue.

4. Scrolling "Phone Slide"
The image of the hand holding the phone should be a scroll-triggered element.

The Motion: As the user scrolls into that section, the phone should slide in from the right side of the screen while the text on the left fades in from the left.

Why: It creates a sense of depth and movement as the user explores the page.

5. Numerical "Count-Up"
For the statistics shown in the "Suur muutus" section (like the numbers next to the bars), use a count-up animation.

Why: It makes the data feel "live" and highlights the growth you are promising.

6. Staggered List Entrance
In the "See on päris vastus" section, don't show all the rows at once.

The Motion: Use a "staggered" fade-in. Row 1 appears, followed by Row 2 (0.1s later), then Row 3, etc.

Why: It encourages the user to read each point rather than being overwhelmed by a block of text.

7. Smooth Accordion FAQ
For the "Korduma kippuvad küsimused" section:

The Motion: When a question is clicked, the answer should slide down using a max-height transition, and the plus/arrow icon should rotate 180 degrees.

Why: Jerky transitions here feel "cheap"; a fluid slide feels professional.

8. Pricing Card "Scale & Highlight"
The pricing cards at the bottom should respond to the mouse.

The Motion: On hover, the card should scale up to 1.05x.

Special Touch: The "Smart Brand" card (the blue one) could have a subtle "shimmer" effect that occasionally passes over the "Vali see pakett" button to draw attention to the preferred choice.

9. Magnetic CTA Buttons
For your primary buttons (the blue ones), implement a "magnetic" hover effect where the button slightly follows the cursor’s movement within its radius.

Why: It’s a trendy, high-end interaction that makes the site feel highly responsive to the user.

10. Sticky "Back to Top" or Progress Bar
Since the page is a long-scroll landing page, add a very thin progress bar at the top of the screen (perhaps 2px high) that fills as the user scrolls down.

Why: It gives the user a visual sense of where they are in the "story" of your sales pitch.
